# WittyReader

WittyReader is a reading app for **EPUB books, PDFs and CBZ comics** that keeps everything on
your own device. It's built for real reading and study:

- a library of your books;
- book view and typography you can set to your own taste;
- highlights with notes and colour meanings;
- handwriting with pen pressure;
- notes you can export and take with you.

## Part of the Witty family

WittyReader is built from three reader plugins, which also power the Books libraries of
[WittyMediaServer](https://github.com/wittywilla/WittyMediaServer):

| Plugin | Reads | Guide |
|---|---|---|
| WittyEbook | EPUB books | [wiki](https://github.com/wittywilla/WittyEbook/wiki) |
| WittyPDF | PDFs | [wiki](https://github.com/wittywilla/WittyPDF/wiki) |
| WittyComics | Comics and manga | [wiki](https://github.com/wittywilla/WittyComics/wiki) |
| WittyInk | Highlights, notes and handwriting for all of them | [wiki](https://github.com/wittywilla/WittyInk/wiki) |

If you want your books and notes on every device (phone, tablet, computer, TV), WittyMediaServer
keeps them on your own server and syncs your place and notes per account.
