# Reading

WittyReader opens each kind of book in its own reader. Here's what each one can do; each reader's
wiki has the full guide.

## Books (EPUB): WittyEbook

**Reading and layout:**

- **Layouts:** book view (two pages with a spine), one page, or scrolling. Auto picks book view
  on wide screens.
- **Turning pages:** tap or click the edges, swipe, or use the arrow keys, Space and Page
  Up/Down.
- **Progress:** a slider for the whole book, and **time left** in the chapter and book from
  your own reading speed.
- **Exact resume:** a book opens on the same words after you change the font or the window size.
- **Contents and footnotes:** the book's contents list, and footnotes in a pop-up.

**Text and display (⚙):**

- **Text:** font (including an extra-readable one), size 60 to 250 %, line spacing, margins,
  justification.
- **Page:** light, sepia, dark or black pages, brightness and a warm night light.

Full guide: [WittyEbook wiki](https://github.com/wittywilla/WittyEbook/wiki).

## PDFs: WittyPDF

- **Zoom:** fit width, fit page, or 50 to 400 % (Ctrl + scroll, **+** and **−**).
- **Layout:** one or two pages across.
- **Page colour:** as printed, sepia or dark pages.
- **Getting around:** the contents list, page thumbnails, search across the document, and go
  to page.
- **Exact resume:** the page and how far down it you were.

Full guide: [WittyPDF wiki](https://github.com/wittywilla/WittyPDF/wiki).

## Comics and manga (CBZ): WittyComics

- **Layouts:** one page, two-page spreads (cover alone), or a vertical strip for webtoons.
- **Direction:** left to right, or right to left for manga. Remembered for each comic, and set
  from `ComicInfo.xml` when it says the comic is manga.
- **Fit:** fit height or fit width.
- **Smooth turning:** the next pages are loaded ahead.

Full guide: [WittyComics wiki](https://github.com/wittywilla/WittyComics/wiki).

## Study tools (books and PDFs)

- **Select text:** highlight in any colour, add a **note**, **copy**, **cite** (with chapter or
  page), **search**, or **read aloud**.
- **Colour meanings:** name what each colour means, such as *Definition* or *Question*.
- **Notes panel ☰:**
  - filter by kind, colour or text;
  - jump to anything you marked;
  - **Export** as Markdown.
- **Bookmarks:** the **bookmark** button or **b**.
- **Undo and redo:** **Ctrl+Z**, **Ctrl+Y**.
- **Definitions:** **Define** appears after you turn on **Settings → Look up words online**.

Full guide: [Highlights and notes](https://github.com/wittywilla/WittyInk/wiki/Highlights-and-notes).

## Handwriting (books and PDFs)

- **Starting:** write with a pen any time, or press the **pen** button (or **p**) to use a finger or mouse.
- **Tools:** pen, pencil, highlighter and eraser (**e**, or the pen's eraser end).
- **Pressure and tilt:** pen pressure sets the width, and the pencil widens with tilt.
- **Colours and sizes:** 36 ink and 18 highlighter colours, thickness and opacity.
- **Palm rejection:** turns on with your first pen stroke.

Full guide: [Handwriting](https://github.com/wittywilla/WittyInk/wiki/Handwriting).
