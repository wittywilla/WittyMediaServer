WittyPlay Online is its own UI, and it still wears your theme
People can switch WittyPlay Online on or off separately from the app's look (Settings, Appearance, Games). Switched off, Games uses the app's look. Switched on, it takes the app's theme so nothing clashes. Its own indigo, coral and teal is its default.
