## Studios that are one studio

### The problem

The owner's example: "Paramount Plus" and "Paramount Plus Amazon Channel" show as two listings. They are the same service, sold through a second store. A person browsing by studio or streaming listing sees two rows of nearly the same titles.

**One thing for the owner to confirm:** the line reads "need to not be combined (if they are closely related like that). No need for different listings, they can be combined (or manually combined in the UI)". This plan reads it as: **closely related names combine into one listing**, and the UI lets a person combine more by hand (and split them again). If the owner meant the opposite, the same machinery runs with the default flipped.

### Where names come from

- Studios and networks from metadata services (company and network records) and from NFO files.
- Streaming listings from the "streaming platform" organisation added in 0.6.0-alpha, which lists what a service currently carries, with the disclosures that we don't stream from it.

The second source is where store-channel variants appear: the same service listed once on its own and again as a channel inside another store.

### The design

**Three layers, applied in order:**

1. **Same record, same listing.** Where a metadata service gives an ID for the company, network or provider, two names with one ID are one listing already. Check this is true everywhere, including NFO-only items.
2. **Known variants.** A small data file shipped with the server, `studio-aliases.json`, maps variant names to one canonical listing. It's data, not code: signed with the server, updated with it, and readable by people. Patterns it covers:
   - The same name sold through a store: "`<Service>` `<Store>` Channel", "`<Service>` via `<Store>`", "`<Service>` on `<Store>`".
   - Plans of one service: "with Ads", "Ad-free", "Basic", "Premium", "Standard".
   - Spellings: "+" and "Plus", "&" and "and", a trailing "TV", punctuation and case.
   - Regions: "(US)", "UK", where the service is the same.
3. **The owner's own combines and splits**, stored per server, which always win over 1 and 2.

**What never combines automatically:** names related only by a parent company. A film studio and the streaming service of the same company stay apart (a person browsing a studio wants the films it made, not everything its owner streams). Same for a network and its streaming service. This is the "closely related like that" line: same service, different store or plan, combines; same company, different business, doesn't.

**Matching rule for layer 2:** normalise (case, accents, punctuation, "+"/"plus", "&"/"and"), strip the store-channel and plan suffixes from the shipped list, and only combine when the remainder matches an existing canonical listing exactly. No fuzzy matching on its own: a 90% string match would merge different companies with similar names. A fuzzy match may only *suggest* (see below).

### The UI

- **Browse by studio, network or streaming listing:** a combined listing shows one card, with "Also listed as: …" on its page.
- **Settings → Metadata → Studios and networks** (or a dialog from the listing's menu): a list of listings with their variants, with:
  - **Combine…**: pick two or more, choose which name and logo the combined one keeps.
  - **Split**: undo a combine, including a shipped one.
  - **Suggestions**: pairs that a fuzzy match thinks are related, for the owner to accept or dismiss. Never applied without a click.
- Combines are per server, admins only, and audited like other metadata changes.
- Works by keyboard, remote and screen reader: the combine flow is a list of checkboxes and a dialog, not drag and drop. Drag and drop can be added as a shortcut.

### Data and safety

- New table `studio_aliases (variant_key, canonical_key, source, created_by, created_at)`. Add it to `tableParts` in `internal/backup/backup.go`, so backups keep it (the tracker's rule for new tables).
- Logos: a combined listing uses the canonical listing's logo, from the optional logo service, with its notice.
- Nothing in the media folders changes. NFO files keep whatever name they had.
- Client API: listings carry `aliases[]`. Existing fields keep their meaning, so old apps show the canonical name and still work.

**Done when:** the owner's example shows one listing out of the box; combine and split work by mouse, keyboard and remote; a backup and restore keeps them; no two different companies combine on the demo library or on `D:\ServerMedia` (read only, `saveMetadata=0`).

## Remote Access that says what is off

### The problem

On **Settings → Remote Access**, the "Only on your home network" choice shows "off" while it is working. "Off" there means "remote access is off", which is what that choice is meant to do, but it reads as "this is broken".

### The rule for the rewrite

**Never a bare "On" or "Off".** Every state names its subject and says what it means for the person:

| Today (roughly) | After |
|---|---|
| Only on your home network · Off | **Home network only.** Devices at home: ✓ connecting. Devices away from home: not allowed, by your choice |
| Remote Access on, but the router isn't forwarding | **Reachable from away: not yet.** Your router isn't sending connections to this server. [What to do] |
| Remote Access on and reachable | **Reachable from away: yes**, at `https://…` (checked 2 minutes ago) |

### The page, top to bottom

1. **A status card** in plain words: who can connect right now, from where, and how it was checked. Green for "working as you chose", amber for "you asked for something that isn't working yet", red only for a real fault. Choosing "home network only" is green: it's working.
2. **The choice:** three cards, each saying who it lets in.
   - **Home network only** (default): "Only devices on your Wi-Fi or cable at home."
   - **Home and away, through your router**
   - **Home and away, through a reverse proxy you already run**
   - (Greyed, as today: WittyRelay, a hosted relay planned as a free service.)
3. **The details** for the chosen card only, with the existing security warnings (`docs/REMOTE.md` keeps "Never forward port 9797" and the rest).
4. **A check button** that tests the path from outside, with the result in words.

### Every surface says the same

The same words in the setup wizard, the tray icon's menu, `GET /api/remote/status` (add `summary` and `subject` fields for apps), WittyMediaClient's connection screen, and the help page. One source of strings, so they can't drift.

**Done when:** a person who has never seen the page can say, from it alone, whether a phone on mobile data can connect, and why. Test that with someone who didn't build it.

## A poster picker you can see

### The problem

Changing a movie's poster opens a selector off screen, and it isn't clear what to do. The owner wants a **pop-up window** that's easy to navigate and see, and the same fix in WittyMediaClient.

### What's there now

From the code as published: the edit dialog (`web/src/components/edit/EditDetails.tsx`) has sections Match, Details, Artwork, Playback, Organise and Logos. The **Artwork** section (`ArtworkSection`) loads candidates from `GET /api/meta/{target}/{id}/artwork?kind=…` and lays them out inside the section, which in a long scrolling dialog can land below the visible area. A separate `ArtworkPicker` dialog exists (`web/src/components/ArtworkPicker.tsx`, "Choose artwork") and is used by the games page only. **To verify:** the exact path that puts the selector off screen, for movies, shows, seasons and episodes.

### The design

- **One picker for every kind of item**, built from `ArtworkPicker`: a modal dialog stacked over the edit dialog (Dialog on Dialog, with the shared `Dialog` from `web/src/ui/Overlay`), centred, and sized to the window.
- **Opens from:** the Artwork section's "Change" button on each image, the item page's poster (menu: Change poster), and the right-click menu in 0.8.0.
- **Inside:**
  - Tabs: Poster, Backdrop, Logo, Banner, Thumb (only those the item has).
  - A grid of candidates, the current one marked, each with its source and size. Language filter for posters with text.
  - **Upload a file** (validated on the server: type sniffed, size limited, decoded and re-encoded before saving, as uploads already are), **paste an address**, or **use a frame from the video** (for episodes and home videos, using the existing thumbnail path).
  - A large preview of the highlighted candidate, at the shape it'll be shown (tall for posters, wide for backdrops).
  - **Apply** and **Cancel**. Apply closes the picker and returns focus to the button that opened it. Nothing is saved until Apply.
- **Keyboard and remote:** arrows move in the grid, Enter previews, a second Enter or the Apply button applies, Escape cancels. Focus is trapped in the picker and returns where it came from.
- **Screen reader:** each candidate is a button named "Poster 3 of 12, from <source>, 1000 by 1500".
- **Phone:** full screen sheet, two columns.
- **Saving beside the media** keeps today's behaviour and notice (poster.jpg or `<name>-poster.jpg`), and follows `saveMetadata`.

### WittyMediaClient

- **Desktop** runs the web app, so it gets the fix with the server. Check it in Electron.
- **Android and Apple:** **to verify** whether the native apps have metadata editing at all. If they do, build the same picker natively (a full-screen grid with D-pad focus on TV) against the same route. If they don't, add "Change poster" to the item's menu for admins, opening the native picker, since the route already exists.

**Done when:** from a movie page, a person can change the poster with mouse, keyboard, TV remote and touch, without scrolling to find anything, and the new poster shows everywhere without a reload.

## Module API 4 and release channels

Full design: [../design/module-release-channels.md](../design/module-release-channels.md).

In short:

- Module API 4 adds a `channel` to each release: **alpha**, **beta** or **stable**.
- A server follows a channel per module (default from the server's own channel), and never moves a module to a less stable channel without the admin choosing it.
- **Every first-party module moves to the alpha channel** with this release, as the owner asked.
- The store shows the channel on each version, a filter by channel, and a switch per module.
- API 3 modules keep working on 0.7.0-alpha as "alpha" (an API 3 release has no channel field, and is treated as alpha), so this doesn't force every module to re-release on day one. **To decide:** whether 0.7.0-alpha keeps `MinAPI` at 3 (unofficial backwards compatibility, as the owner allowed) or raises it to 4. This plan recommends keeping 3 for 0.7.x and raising it at 0.8.0.

## Preferred audio languages, per person

The owner, 2026-11-02: "allow you to set a preferred audio language (if available)... allow the user to prefer a language or enforce a language requirement." Today there's one server-wide text box. 0.7.0-alpha gives each profile an ordered list (with **Original language**), the same chooser in every player and app, and choices remembered per show and film on the server. Enforcing a language (refuse, ask, filter, channel rules) follows in the next major. Full design: [../design/audio-language-preferences.md](../design/audio-language-preferences.md).

## Keeping a title off TV channels

The owner, 2026-11-02: "allow individual listings (like a DVD for instance) to be unavailable for the TV Guide to use (for instance, my 3d blu ray)". A **Keep off TV channels** switch on any film, disc, show, season or episode, honoured by every channel's rules, one-click channels and disc lists, with a deliberate per-channel exception. 3D titles are left out of channels by default, since a channel's shared 2D stream can't show them. Full design: [../design/keep-off-channels.md](../design/keep-off-channels.md).

## One-click updates for Windows installs

The owner, 2026-11-02: updating isn't obvious in the UI. 0.6.3-alpha makes updates easy to find and adds a button that downloads the right installer. 0.7.0-alpha makes that button install it on Windows: the server downloads and verifies the MSI, makes a backup, then the tray app runs it after Windows' admin prompt, or the service runs it itself after the admin's password in the page. It never starts while someone's watching, recording or playing unless the admin says so, and "Update tonight" is offered. Needs the signed MSI from the code signing work to pass Smart App Control. Full design: [../design/server-updates.md](../design/server-updates.md).
