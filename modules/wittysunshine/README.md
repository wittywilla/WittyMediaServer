<h1 align="center">WittySunshine</h1>

<p align="center">
  <b>An advanced option: host demanding PC games on your server's own hardware.</b>
</p>

> **Not an official project.** WittySunshine isn't part of, or made by, the [Sunshine](https://github.com/LizardByte/Sunshine)
> project (LizardByte). It contains none of its code.

> Read-only mirror of `modules/wittysunshine` in [WittyMediaServer](https://github.com/wittywilla/WittyMediaServer),
> where development happens. Please open issues and pull requests there.

## What it does

Most servers don't need this. To play on other PCs, a friend's included, that's [WittyMoonlight](https://github.com/wittywilla/WittyMoonlight).

For a server with a good graphics card and PC games installed on it, this finds a separately installed Sunshine on that machine, so its games can
become a library. It's off until an admin turns it on. People play through WittyMoonlight, as with any other PC.
