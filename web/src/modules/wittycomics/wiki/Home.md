# WittyComics

WittyComics is the Witty reader for **comics, manga and webtoons**. Read a page at a time, as
two-page spreads, or as one long strip, left to right or right to left.

You'll find it in:

- **[WittyMediaServer](https://github.com/wittywilla/WittyMediaServer):** open a comic from a
  Books library and choose **Read**. Your place follows you to every device.
- **[WittyReader](https://github.com/wittywilla/WittyReader/wiki):** open CBZ files on your own
  device.

## Guide

- [Reading comics](Reading-comics): layouts, direction, fit, and every way to turn pages.
- [Embedding](Embedding): for developers adding WittyComics to an app.

## Not supported yet

- Highlights, notes and handwriting on comic pages. They're available in
  [WittyEbook](https://github.com/wittywilla/WittyEbook/wiki) and
  [WittyPDF](https://github.com/wittywilla/WittyPDF/wiki).
- Guided view (zooming panel by panel).
