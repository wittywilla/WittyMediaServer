# WittyEbook

WittyEbook is the Witty reader for **reflowable ebooks** (EPUB 2 and 3). Reflowable means the
text flows to fit your screen and your settings, unlike a PDF's fixed pages. You choose the font,
size, spacing and page colour, and read in book view, one page at a time, or scrolling.

You'll find it in:

- **[WittyMediaServer](https://github.com/wittywilla/WittyMediaServer):** open a book from a
  Books library and choose **Read**. Your place and notes follow you to every device.
- **[WittyReader](https://github.com/wittywilla/WittyReader/wiki):** open EPUB files on your own
  device.

## Guide

- [Reading and layout](Reading-and-layout): book view, turning pages, progress and time left,
  contents, footnotes, resuming.
- [Text and display](Text-and-display): fonts, size, spacing, margins, page colours, brightness
  and night light.
- [Study tools](Study-tools): highlights, notes, bookmarks, search, definitions, read aloud,
  citations and handwriting.
- [Keyboard shortcuts](Keyboard-shortcuts)
- [Embedding](Embedding): for developers adding WittyEbook to an app.

The study tools and handwriting are shared with WittyPDF. Their full guides are in the
[WittyInk wiki](https://github.com/wittywilla/WittyInk/wiki).

## Not supported yet

- Fixed-layout EPUBs (picture books laid out page by page) open, but reflow like other books.
- Books with copy protection (DRM) can't be opened.
- Ebook formats other than EPUB.
