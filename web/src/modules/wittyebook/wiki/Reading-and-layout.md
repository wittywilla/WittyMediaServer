# Reading and layout

## The screen

- **Top bar:** back, the book's title and current chapter, and buttons for:
  - Contents
  - Search
  - Notes ☰
  - Handwriting ✎
  - Bookmark 🔖
  - Read aloud 🔊
  - Text and display ⚙
- **Footer:** previous and next page, a slider for the whole book, and the percentage read with
  time left in the chapter and in the book.

Both bars hide after a few seconds so only the page shows. To bring them back, tap the middle of
the page, or move the mouse near the top or bottom edge. While they're hidden, a small
percentage stays at the bottom.

## Layouts

Choose in **⚙ → Layout**:

| Layout | What you see |
|---|---|
| **Auto** (default) | Book view when the window is at least 1000 pixels wide and wider than it is tall; one page otherwise. |
| **One page** | A single page, sized to the window. |
| **Book view** | Two pages side by side with a soft spine down the middle, like an open book. |
| **Scroll** | One long column per chapter that you scroll. At the end of a chapter, the next-page key or a tap on the right edge opens the next one. |

## Turning pages

- **Click or tap:** the left quarter of the page goes back, the right quarter goes forward, and
  the middle shows or hides the bars.
- **Swipe** left or right on a touch screen.
- **Keys:** → Space Page Down go forward; ← Page Up go back.
- **‹ ›** in the footer.

Turning past the last page of a chapter opens the next one. Going back from a chapter's first
page opens the end of the previous one. Each turn has a short slide; it's switched off if your
system asks for reduced motion.

## Progress and time left

- The **slider** spans the whole book, measured in words, so long chapters take more of it.
  Drag it to jump anywhere.
- **Time left** comes from your own pace. While you read, WittyEbook times your page turns
  (ignoring quick flips and long breaks) and works out your words per minute. Until it knows your
  pace, it assumes 250 words a minute.

## Resuming

Your place is saved as you read: the chapter and how far into its text you are. Because it's a
place in the text, not a page number, the book opens on the same words after you change the font
size, rotate a tablet, or switch to another device. In WittyMediaServer it's saved to your
account.

## Contents

**Contents** in the top bar lists the book's table of contents, including sections inside
chapters. The chapter you're in is marked. If a book has no table of contents, its chapters are
listed as *Section 1*, *Section 2*, and so on.

## Footnotes and links

- **Footnotes** (links marked as note references by the book, or short links like *1*, *[2]*,
  *\**, *†*) open in a **pop-up** with the note's text. **Go to the note** takes you to it if you
  want the full context.
- **Links to other parts of the book** take you there.
- **Web links** open in a new tab. The book itself never navigates away.

## Bookmarks

**🔖** (or **b**) bookmarks the page, and a red ribbon appears in the top corner. Press it again
to remove the bookmark. Find bookmarks in the notes panel ☰.

## Images

Pictures are scaled down to fit within a page, so a tall picture is never split across two
pages.
