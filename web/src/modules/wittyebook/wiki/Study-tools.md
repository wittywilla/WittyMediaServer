# Study tools

WittyEbook includes the full Witty study toolkit. This page covers how each tool behaves in
books; the shared guides in the WittyInk wiki go into more detail:

- [Highlights and notes](https://github.com/wittywilla/WittyInk/wiki/Highlights-and-notes)
- [Handwriting](https://github.com/wittywilla/WittyInk/wiki/Handwriting)

## Highlights and notes

- **Select text** for the toolbar:
  - four quick colours, and **+** for 18 more or any colour;
  - **Note**, **Define** (up to three words), **Copy**, **Cite**, **Search**, **Read aloud**.
- **Highlights are tied to the words.** Each one remembers its text and a little of the text
  around it, so it lands on the right words after you change the font or layout, and even when
  the same phrase appears twice in a chapter.
- **Click a highlight** to change its colour, add a note, cite it or remove it. Highlights with
  a note have a dotted underline.
- **Colour meanings, the notes panel ☰, its filters and Markdown export** are described in
  [Highlights and notes](https://github.com/wittywilla/WittyInk/wiki/Highlights-and-notes).

## Search

Search with the 🔍 button, **/**, or **Search** on a selection. Notes:

- Searches need at least two characters and ignore upper and lower case.
- The whole book is searched, not just the open chapter.
- Results show the chapter and the words around each match, up to 200 matches.
- Choosing a result opens that chapter at the match, which flashes orange for a moment.

## Definitions

**Define** on a selected word shows its pronunciation, parts of speech, meanings and examples,
with a link to Wiktionary. The button appears when the app provides a dictionary:

- **WittyMediaServer** looks words up through your server.
- **WittyReader** looks them up only after you turn it on in its settings.

## Read aloud

- **🔊** reads from the first paragraph on the page, using your device's voices in the book's
  language.
- It turns pages as it goes and carries on into the next chapter.
- **Read aloud** on a selection starts from that paragraph.
- Press **🔊** again to stop.

## Citations

**Cite** copies the selection with the author, title, year and chapter:

> “Call me Ishmael.” (Herman Melville, Moby Dick; Or, The Whale (1851), CHAPTER 1. Loomings)

## Handwriting

Write with a pen at any time, or press the **pen** button (or **p**) to use a finger or mouse. In books:

- **Strokes stay with their words.** Each stroke is tied to the word it starts next to, and sized
  by the book's text size, so a circled word stays circled when the text reflows.
- **The highlighter snaps to text.** A highlighter line drawn along a line of text becomes a real
  text highlight, extended to whole words. It works like any other highlight: notes, colour
  meanings, citations. Longer or curved highlighter strokes stay as ink. You can turn snapping
  off in the ink toolbar's **⋯** settings.
- **Fingers turn pages** while palm rejection is on.

See [Handwriting](https://github.com/wittywilla/WittyInk/wiki/Handwriting) for the tools,
colours, pressure and eraser.

## Undo

**Ctrl+Z** and **Ctrl+Y** undo and redo adding or removing highlights and handwriting.
