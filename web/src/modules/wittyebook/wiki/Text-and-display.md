# Text and display

Open **⚙ Text and display** in the top bar. Changes apply at once, and the page keeps your
place as the text reflows.

| Setting | Choices |
|---|---|
| **Layout** | Auto, One page, Book view, Scroll (see [Reading and layout](Reading-and-layout)). |
| **Font** | **Publisher's font** (the book's own design), **Serif** (Georgia-style), **Sans-serif** (your system's interface font), **Extra readable** (wide, open letters such as Verdana, often easier with dyslexia or low vision), **Monospace**. |
| **Size** | 60 % to 250 %, with **A−** and **A+** for quick steps. |
| **Line spacing** | From tight (1.1) to airy (2.2); 1.55 to start. |
| **Margins** | Seven widths, from edge to edge to generous. |
| **Justify and hyphenate** | Even edges with words split at line ends. Turn it off for ragged-right text, which some readers find easier. |
| **Page** | Light, sepia, dark or black. |
| **Brightness** | Dims the page down to 35 %, without touching your screen's own brightness. Handy at night or on a projector. |
| **Warmth** | A warm amber layer that cuts blue light, as a night light. |
| **Reset** | Back to the starting settings. |

Notes:

- Brightness and warmth are layers over the page; the book itself never changes.
- Your choices are remembered on this device and used for every book.
- Highlights and handwriting stay with their words whatever you change.
