# Handwriting

Write in the margins, circle words, underline, sketch diagrams. Handwriting works in
[WittyEbook](https://github.com/wittywilla/WittyEbook/wiki) and
[WittyPDF](https://github.com/wittywilla/WittyPDF/wiki) with a pen, a finger or a mouse.

## Starting to write

- **With a pen:** just write on the page. A pen writes at any time, without opening any tools
  (you can turn this off).
- **With a finger or mouse:** press the **pen** button in the top bar (or the **p** key) to open the ink
  toolbar. While it is open, dragging writes instead of selecting text.
- Press **Done** (or **Esc**) to close the toolbar.

## The ink toolbar

| Control | What it does |
|---|---|
| ✒ **Pen** | A smooth line whose width follows pen pressure. |
| ✏ **Pencil** | A thinner line with a light grain. It gets wider when you tilt the pen. |
| 🖍 **Highlighter** | A wide, see-through line. On light pages it blends with the text instead of covering it. |
| ⌫ **Eraser** | Removes a whole stroke when you touch it (**e** key). |
| **Colour dot** | Opens the palette for the current tool: 36 ink colours (pen and pencil) or 18 highlighter colours, plus any colour you pick. |
| **Small dots next to it** | Your recently used colours. |
| **Thickness** | Six sizes per tool: pen 0.75 to 9 px, pencil 0.5 to 6 px, highlighter 8 to 40 px. |
| ↶ ↷ | Undo and redo (**Ctrl+Z**, **Ctrl+Y**). |
| **⋯** | Pen settings (below). |
| ‹ › | Turn the page without closing the toolbar. |

Each tool keeps its own colour, thickness and opacity, and they're remembered on the device.
When the app is opened again it starts with a writing tool, never the eraser.

## Pen settings (⋯)

- **Thickness:** exact width, from 0.25 to 16 px (highlighter: 4 to 60 px).
- **Opacity:** 10 to 100 %.
- **Pen pressure changes thickness:** on by default. Turn it off for an even line.
- **Only a pen draws (fingers turn pages):** palm rejection. It turns itself on the first time a
  pen touches the page, so a hand resting on the screen leaves no marks. With it on:
  - in books, finger swipes turn pages;
  - in PDFs, fingers move the page.
- **Draw with a pen any time:** when off, a pen selects text like a mouse until you open the ink
  toolbar.
- **Highlighter over text makes a text highlight:** in books, a highlighter line drawn across a
  line of text becomes a real text highlight covering the whole words, with notes and colour
  meanings like any other. In PDFs the highlighter always draws ink; select text to make a text
  highlight there.

## Pens with an eraser

- **Eraser end:** turn the pen over and rub out strokes.
- **Side (barrel) button:** hold it while touching the page to erase.

## Pressure and tilt

- **Pressure:** the line widens as you press harder, from about a third of the chosen thickness
  up to about 1.7 times it. A mouse or finger draws at the chosen thickness.
- **Tilt:** the pencil widens as the pen leans over, like the side of a lead.

## Where strokes stay

- **In books**, text reflows when you change the font or window size. Each stroke is tied to the
  word it starts next to, and its size is measured in the book's text size. A note circled in the
  margin stays next to its paragraph and grows with the text.
- **In PDFs** pages never reflow, so strokes are kept in the page's own units. They stay exactly
  where you drew them at any zoom.

## Shortcuts

| Key | Action |
|---|---|
| **p** | Open or close the ink toolbar. |
| **e** | Eraser (while the toolbar is open). |
| **Ctrl+Z** | Undo. |
| **Ctrl+Y** or **Ctrl+Shift+Z** | Redo. |
| **Esc** | Close the toolbar. |
