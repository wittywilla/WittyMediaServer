# WittyPDF

WittyPDF is the Witty reader for **PDFs**: papers, textbooks, manuals and scans. Pages look
exactly as printed, the text can be selected, and you can highlight, take notes and write on any
page with a pen.

You'll find it in:

- **[WittyMediaServer](https://github.com/wittywilla/WittyMediaServer):** open a PDF from a Books
  library and choose **Read**. Your place and notes follow you to every device.
- **[WittyReader](https://github.com/wittywilla/WittyReader/wiki):** open PDF files on your own
  device.

## Not supported yet

- Password-protected PDFs.
- Links inside a PDF (for example to a reference or a web page) can't be clicked yet. Use the
  contents list and search to move around.
- Filling in PDF forms.
- Saving notes into the PDF file itself. Notes are kept by the app, and can be exported as
  Markdown.
