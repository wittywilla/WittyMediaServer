# Study tools and handwriting

WittyPDF has the full Witty study toolkit. This page covers how each tool behaves in PDFs; the
shared guides in the WittyInk wiki go into more detail:

- [Highlights and notes](https://github.com/wittywilla/WittyInk/wiki/Highlights-and-notes)
- [Handwriting](https://github.com/wittywilla/WittyInk/wiki/Handwriting)

## Highlights

- **Select text** with the mouse, a finger or a pen, then pick a colour in the toolbar (or **+**
  for more).
- A highlight is drawn as **boxes over each line** you selected. A selection that runs onto the
  next page is highlighted on both pages.
- **Click a highlight** to change its colour, write a note, copy a citation or remove it.
  Highlights with a note have a line along their bottom edge.
- Highlights are stored as positions on the page, so they stay put at any zoom, in one or two
  pages across, and in dark mode.

## Notes panel

**☰** lists highlights, notes, bookmarks and handwritten pages in page order, each with its
*Page N*:

- filter by kind, colour or text;
- name your colours;
- jump to any entry;
- **Export** saves everything as Markdown, grouped by page.

## Citations

**Cite** copies the selection with its page number:

> “Adaptive buffering reduces stalls on low-end devices.” (A. Example, Adaptive Buffering for
> Home Media Servers (2026), p. 5)

## Bookmarks

**🔖** (or **b**) bookmarks the page at the top of the screen, and a red ribbon appears on that
page. Press it again to remove the bookmark.

## Definitions

**Define** on a selected word (up to three words) shows the dictionary entry, when the app
provides a dictionary.

## Read aloud

- **🔊** reads from the page you're on, sentence by sentence, and moves on through the pages.
- **Read aloud** on a selection starts from that sentence.
- Press **🔊** again to stop. It uses your device's voices.

## Handwriting

- **Starting:** write with a pen at any time, or press **✎** (or **p**) to write with a finger or
  mouse.
- **Strokes are stored in the page's own units**, so they stay exactly where you drew them at any
  zoom or layout.
- **Palm rejection:** while it's on (it turns on with your first pen stroke), fingers **move the
  page** and only the pen writes, even with the handwriting tools open.
- **The highlighter** draws a see-through stroke over the page. To make a text highlight with
  notes and citations, select the text instead.
- **Erasing:** the **eraser** (**e**), the pen's eraser end, or its side button.

Tools, colours, pressure and thickness are described in
[Handwriting](https://github.com/wittywilla/WittyInk/wiki/Handwriting).

## Undo

**Ctrl+Z** and **Ctrl+Y** undo and redo adding or removing highlights and handwriting.
