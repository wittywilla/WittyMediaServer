# Viewing and zoom

## The screen

- **Top bar:** back, the title and author, the zoom controls, and buttons for:
  - Contents and pages
  - Search
  - Notes ☰
  - Handwriting ✎
  - Bookmark 🔖
  - Read aloud 🔊
  - Display ⚙
- **Pages:** one continuous scroll.
- **Footer:** previous and next page, a page slider, and a box to type a page number.

The bars hide after a few seconds. Click an empty part of a page, or move the mouse near the top
or bottom edge, to show them again.

## Zoom

| How | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Fit width** (the default) | Pages fill the window's width. Best for reading papers. |
| **Fit page** | A whole page fits on screen. Best for slides and posters. |
| The **Fit width / Fit page** button | Switches between the two. While a set zoom is in use it shows the percentage. |
| **−** and **+** in the top bar, the **−** and **+** keys | Step through 50, 67, 80, 100, 125, 150, 200, 300 and 400 %. From a fitted zoom they go to the next step up or down. |
| **Ctrl + scroll wheel** (or a trackpad pinch) | Zooms the document, not the whole app. |
| **Ctrl+=**, **Ctrl+−**, **Ctrl+0** | Zoom in, zoom out, back to fit width. |
| **⚙ → Zoom** | Fit width, Fit page, 100 %, 150 % or 200 %. |

Zooming keeps you at the same place on the page. Your zoom is remembered on the device for every
PDF.

## One or two pages across

**⚙ → Pages across → Two** shows pages side by side, like an open book. That's good for wide
screens and documents designed as spreads. With two across, the next and previous page buttons
move a whole row.

## Page colour

**⚙ → Page colour**:

- **As printed:** the document's own colours.
- **Sepia:** a warm, softer page.
- **Dark:** light text on dark pages, for reading at night. Pictures change colour too, so switch
  back to read a chart or photo.

Highlights and handwriting keep their own colours on dark pages.

## Text

Every page has a text layer, so you can select and copy text, and highlight it. Scanned pages
without text (pictures of paper) can still be read and written on, but have no text to select.
