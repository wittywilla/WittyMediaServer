# Using it

**Turning it on.** In the audio player, press the bars button, or **V**. The first time, there's a notice about flashing and a
choice of calm mode. It's off again next time you load the page, unless you ticked **Show it when I open the full player**.

**Where it shows.** On the whole screen from the bar along the bottom, in place of the artwork in the full player, and small in
place of the thumbnail once you leave that. In WittyMediaPlayer, it fills the window while music plays.

| Key | What it does |
|---|---|
| **←** **→**, or **P** **N** | Previous and next effect |
| **R** | Random order on or off |
| **S** | Mark this effect as a favourite |
| **F** | Full screen |
| **+** **−** | How strongly it reacts |
| **V** | Off |
| **Esc** | Out of full screen |

With a remote or controller: left and right change the effect, up, down or OK reach the controls, and Back leaves. On a
desktop app, the arrows change the effect while the picture has the focus or it's on the whole screen, and otherwise they
seek as usual.

**Settings** (the gear): how often the effect changes, random order, favourites only, how strongly it reacts, how easily it
hears the music, calm mode, resolution, and showing the frame rate. They follow you between devices on a server, as your other
interface choices do. On the desktop they stay on that computer.

**If it stutters.** Leave resolution on Automatic and it finds its own level. If that's not enough, choose a lower one
yourself. On a device with no graphics acceleration it starts low, at 30 frames a second.

**If it says it's following a pattern.** It can't read the sound here, usually because the server is on a different address from
the page, or (on the desktop) because the VLC libraries are playing. The music is fine. The picture just isn't following it.
